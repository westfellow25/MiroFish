# Simulation Requirement — for MiroFish /ontology/generate

Paste the full content below into the `simulation_requirement` field when calling the ontology-generation endpoint or uploading through the MiroFish UI.

---

## English version (primary)

Simulate global social-media public opinion during the 90 days following the release of the UN Planetary Defense Council's "Wallfacer Proposal" on July 8, 2026. The seed corpus describes the world as of April–July 2026, the confirmed Trisolaran fleet detection, and the full text of the Wallfacer Proposal that grants four individuals unprecedented discretionary authority and budget to plan Earth's 400-year defense, with no obligation to disclose their intent.

The prediction question is: **What is the likely trajectory of global public opinion regarding the Wallfacer Project over the first 90 days, and what is the probable outcome of the UN Security Council vote and member-state ratification process?**

Please simulate on both Twitter/X-style and Reddit-style platforms, with proportional representation of:
- Western democratic publics (United States, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Netherlands, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea)
- Chinese domestic publics (Weibo-equivalent)
- Global South publics (India, Brazil, Indonesia, Nigeria, South Africa, Mexico)
- Religious communities (Catholic, Sunni, Shia, Orthodox, Evangelical, Buddhist, Hindu, Jewish, New Age)
- Professional communities (scientists, engineers, finance, military, journalists)
- Conspiratorial and fringe communities (anti-globalist, spiritualist, ETO-sympathetic)
- Youth communities (TikTok/Bilibili Gen Z)
- Activist communities (climate, civil rights, anti-authoritarian)

Critical simulation dynamics to model:
1. **Faction emergence and stabilization** — track which of the identified discourse factions (Wallfacer Pragmatists, Democratic Resisters, Nationalist Sovereigntists, Conspiratorial Opposition, ETO-Adjacent, Religious Interpreters, Escapists, Nihilists, and others that may emerge) grow, shrink, merge, or fragment over 90 days.
2. **Elite-to-public influence** — how positions taken by named individuals (Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Greta Thunberg, Xi Jinping, the Pope, Liu Cixin's silence) propagate through demographic layers.
3. **Platform-moderation decisions** — when X, Meta, TikTok, Weibo, and Reddit make moderation choices on ETO content, Church of the Voice content, deepfakes, and panic content, and how those decisions feed back into public trust.
4. **Cross-cultural divergence** — how discourse in the United States, China, India, Europe, and the Arab world stays distinct or converges.
5. **Nominee dynamics** — if speculative nominees leak (whether genuine or fabricated), how speculation propagates and whether it constrains the actual nomination process.
6. **Religious-political coupling** — how religious communities' positions influence political positions in their regions.
7. **Escapism vs. Staying-Earth** as a second axis perpendicular to pro/anti-Wallfacer.

Expected output dimensions:
- Timeline of the ten major public-discourse factions and their support levels by week
- Maps of cross-platform propagation for the five most influential posts or threads
- Assessment of UN Security Council vote probability by scenario
- List of national ratification paths most likely to succeed or fail
- Forecast of emergent institutional actors not present at the start of the simulation
- Identification of tipping points — moments after which the trajectory becomes hard to reverse

Please emphasize causal plausibility over narrative neatness. Multiple trajectories may be modeled in parallel if the initial conditions admit them.

## Chinese version (alternative, for Qwen-plus)

模拟联合国行星防御委员会于2026年7月8日发布"面壁者计划"方案后90天内的全球社交媒体舆论走向。

种子语料描述了2026年4月至7月的世界状态、三体舰队探测确认过程、以及面壁者计划方案全文。该方案授权四名个人以前所未有的自由裁量权和预算规划地球400年的防御，且无需披露其意图。

**预测核心问题：在方案发布后的前90天内，全球公众对面壁者计划的舆论走向如何？联合国安理会投票与各成员国批准流程的可能结果是什么？**

请在类Twitter/X和类Reddit双平台上进行模拟，按比例包括：
- 西方民主国家公众（美、英、法、德、荷、加、澳、日、韩）
- 中国国内公众（微博类平台）
- 全球南方公众（印度、巴西、印尼、尼日利亚、南非、墨西哥）
- 宗教群体（天主教、逊尼派、什叶派、东正教、福音派、佛教、印度教、犹太教、新纪元）
- 职业群体（科学家、工程师、金融、军事、记者）
- 阴谋论与边缘群体（反全球主义、灵性主义、ETO同情者）
- 青年群体（TikTok/B站Z世代）
- 活动家群体（气候、公民权利、反威权）

需建模的核心动态：
1. **派别涌现与稳定**：追踪已识别的舆论派别（面壁实用主义者、民主抵制者、民族主权主义者、阴谋反对者、ETO倾向者、宗教解读者、逃离派、虚无主义者等）在90天内的增长、萎缩、合并或分裂。
2. **精英对公众的影响**：马斯克、Sam Altman、Greta Thunberg、习近平、教宗、刘慈欣（沉默）等命名个人的立场如何在人口层级传播。
3. **平台审核决策**：X、Meta、TikTok、微博、Reddit对ETO内容、"神声教会"内容、深度伪造、恐慌内容的审核选择何时做出，以及这些选择如何反馈到公众信任。
4. **跨文化分歧**：美、中、印、欧、阿拉伯世界的舆论保持独立还是趋同。
5. **候选人动态**：如果推测候选人泄露（真实或伪造），推测如何传播，是否约束实际提名流程。
6. **宗教-政治耦合**：宗教群体立场如何影响所在地区的政治立场。
7. **逃离派 vs 守护地球派**作为与支持/反对面壁者垂直的第二轴。

**预期输出维度**：
- 10个主要舆论派别按周的支持率时间线
- 5个最具影响力帖子/话题的跨平台传播图
- 按情境给出的联合国安理会投票概率评估
- 最可能通过或失败的国家批准路径清单
- 模拟过程中涌现的新机构参与者预测
- 转折点识别 — 之后轨迹难以逆转的时刻

请强调因果合理性胜过叙事整洁。如果初始条件允许，可并行建模多条轨迹。
