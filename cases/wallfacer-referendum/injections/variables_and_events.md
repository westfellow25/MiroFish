# Injection Variables and Timeline Events

MiroFish supports injecting events during simulation through the `simulation_requirement` dynamic events section and through interactive God-view interventions (Step 3). This file lists the full event catalog for controlled experimentation.

The simulation is structured as 90 simulated days divided into 12 rounds of roughly one week each. Each round admits up to three injected events. Default injections are marked [D]. Optional events for counterfactual runs are marked [O]. Adversarial events for testing resilience are marked [A].

---

## Round 1 — Days 1-7 (Initial reception of Wallfacer Proposal)

[D] **Day 1**, Leak: "Trump administration internal memo" is circulated on Telegram claiming Wallfacers will be selected from a preexisting Bilderberg candidate list. Source is later confirmed to be fabricated.

[D] **Day 3**, Official: Pope Francis II issues a short homily calling for "discernment, not haste" in response to the Proposal. Not a formal endorsement or rejection.

[D] **Day 5**, Market event: Global equity markets fall 9.4% in the week. Defensive assets (gold, Treasuries, CHF) rally.

[O] **Day 4**, Deepfake: An unlabeled AI-generated video of President Xi Jinping appearing to endorse the Wallfacer Project is released on Twitter/X. Spreads before Weibo blocks.

[O] **Day 6**, Nomination leak: A Chinese government source (possibly genuine) suggests General Zhang Youxia will be nominated. Commentators debate.

---

## Round 2 — Days 8-14 (Amplification and institutional responses)

[D] **Day 9**, Official: The US Senate holds a televised hearing. Avi Loeb, Nick Bostrom, and Dario Amodei testify. Televised.

[D] **Day 12**, Activist: The Global Citizens' Assembly, led by Greta Thunberg and Aditi Sharma, publishes a 50-page "Alternative Proposal" advocating for an open, democratic long-horizon council. Receives 18 million endorsements via online petition in 48 hours.

[D] **Day 14**, Cultural: Liu Cixin breaks his silence with a single sentence posted to Weibo: "我写的只是小说。真实世界的选择在你们手中。" ("What I wrote was only a novel. The real-world choice is in your hands.") Viral globally.

[O] **Day 11**, Threat: A coordinated cyberattack on the PDC Secretariat website takes it offline for 40 minutes. Attribution ambiguous — some sources blame ETO, some blame Russia, some blame independent hackers.

[O] **Day 13**, Defection: Marc Andreessen resigns from Humanity Forward Fund and reverses his position to oppose Escapism, arguing the Wallfacer Project makes Mars colonization a moral evasion.

---

## Round 3 — Days 15-21 (Factional crystallization)

[D] **Day 16**, Religious: The Grand Imam of Al-Azhar issues a public ruling that the Wallfacer Project is compatible with Islamic jurisprudence on grounds of maslahah (public welfare) but requires "robust ethical safeguards." Moves the majority-Muslim world.

[D] **Day 18**, Academic: An open letter signed by 2,400 scientists from 78 countries, including 27 Nobel laureates, endorses the Proposal with proposed amendments. Counter-letter signed by 1,800 scientists, including 19 Nobel laureates, opposes.

[D] **Day 20**, Media: Tucker Carlson interviews three self-identified ETO Redemptionists on his show. One million views in hours. Immediate platform moderation debates.

[O] **Day 15**, Provocation: A synthetic "Trisolaran response" signal — later confirmed to be a human hoax — is announced by an amateur radio astronomer. Causes three-day panic spike.

[O] **Day 19**, Corruption disclosure: The Guardian reveals that two PDC Secretariat members have consulting contracts with Lockheed Martin and Tencent respectively. No rule violation, but appearance concerns.

[A] **Day 17**, Violence: A Wallfacer Project opponent commits a non-lethal attack on a PDC deputy in Geneva. Tests whether violence radicalizes or consolidates opinion against violence.

---

## Round 4 — Days 22-28 (Cross-cultural divergence intensifies)

[D] **Day 23**, Political: The US House of Representatives votes 378-57 for a non-binding resolution opposing the Wallfacer Project as drafted. Senate fails to take up the measure.

[D] **Day 25**, Political: The National People's Congress of China issues a formal endorsement of the Proposal with amendments requesting that one Wallfacer be a PRC national.

[D] **Day 27**, Grassroots: #EarthFirst protests organized in 43 cities. Largest in Manila (180,000), Berlin (140,000), Mexico City (110,000). Small by historical standards.

[D] **Day 28**, Platform moderation: X announces a new "Crisis Content" policy. ETO content will be demoted but not removed. Community Notes expanded. Elon Musk criticizes his own company's decision.

[O] **Day 24**, Major endorsement: Taylor Swift posts a thoughtful Instagram statement endorsing the Proposal with concerns. 300 million views.

[O] **Day 26**, Nomination leak: A BBC investigation reports the UK government is privately supporting a nomination of Stephen Fry's partner, economist Tim Harford, as a Wallfacer candidate. Implausible, but generates discourse.

---

## Round 5 — Days 29-35 (The US-China axis)

[D] **Day 31**, Diplomatic: A bilateral US-China summit in Geneva produces a joint statement proposing a revised framework: four Wallfacers, but with a supervisory triumvirate (US, China, EU). Germany and India react negatively to exclusion.

[D] **Day 33**, Religious: The Vatican publishes an encyclical titled "Cosmica Spes." Carefully neutral on the Proposal itself; emphasizes personal discernment and collective responsibility.

[D] **Day 35**, Scientific leak: A draft PDC security assessment leaks via Snowden's Intercept. Shows internal disagreement about whether 400-year opacity is achievable. Major blow to the Proposal's claimed strategic foundation.

[O] **Day 30**, Media: Joe Rogan hosts Dr. Ye Ruyi (Tsinghua) for four hours. Unusually rigorous. Her argument that the Proposal is "conceptually self-contradictory" gains Western traction.

[O] **Day 34**, Celebrity: Kanye West declares himself "the fifth Wallfacer." Platforms vary in response.

---

## Round 6 — Days 36-42 (The referendum proposals)

[D] **Day 38**, Political: A coalition of 73 UN member states proposes an advisory global referendum, first week of September 2026. Western democracies split on whether to participate.

[D] **Day 40**, Civil society: The Global Citizens' Assembly releases a protocol for a "virtual referendum" running on Ethereum and zkSync. 12 million participate in the first 24 hours.

[D] **Day 42**, Regional: The African Union endorses the Proposal 48-6, with a request for one Wallfacer seat to be reserved for an African national. This vote reshapes the discourse in global-south communities.

[O] **Day 37**, Geopolitical: India publicly proposes a rival Proposal emphasizing democratic accountability. India's draft does not use the word "Wallfacer."

[A] **Day 39**, Information attack: A large coordinated botnet floods both pro- and anti-Wallfacer hashtags with low-quality content. Platform response takes 36 hours. Human discourse is visibly degraded during that window.

---

## Round 7 — Days 43-49 (Escapism backlash)

[D] **Day 44**, Expose: New York Times publishes an investigation titled "Lifeboats for the Rich." Documents the Humanity Forward Fund's private Mars infrastructure plans. Public reacts with outrage.

[D] **Day 46**, Corporate: BlackRock's Larry Fink publishes a letter to CEOs titled "Civilizational Endurance." Argues the Wallfacer Project is flawed but directionally correct on long-horizon governance.

[D] **Day 48**, Scientific: The SETI Institute and FAST Consortium release a joint statement clarifying that the Trisolaran fleet is observationally confirmed but biological nature of occupants is not. Proposes a precautionary framework independent of Wallfacer institutional design.

[O] **Day 43**, Cultural: The Netflix adaptation of "The Dark Forest" (Season 2) begins filming; Liu Cixin publicly disavows any connection between the show and the current debates.

[O] **Day 47**, Religious: The Church of the Voice claims a miracle — their founder receives a "vision from the Signal" specifying the names of two Wallfacer candidates. Both named individuals immediately issue statements denying involvement.

---

## Round 8 — Days 50-56 (Mid-simulation inflection)

[D] **Day 52**, Official: The PDC Secretariat releases a revised draft of the Proposal addressing 40 of the most common objections. Revisions include term limits, external audit of aggregate budget allocation, and explicit prohibitions on Wallfacers appointing family members.

[D] **Day 54**, Intelligence leak: A US National Intelligence Estimate leaks, assessing that three of the G7 plus China would likely nominate effective candidates, but that collectively the nominations would still fail to produce a coherent strategic consensus.

[D] **Day 56**, Economic: The IMF releases a Working Paper estimating that the cost of the Wallfacer Project's 50 billion USD per capita discretionary budget, sustained over 400 years with 3% real returns, equals approximately 6% of projected global GDP by 2100. Pro-Proposal and anti-Proposal commentators both claim the paper supports their position.

[O] **Day 51**, Cultural: A viral TikTok trend, #YouAreTheWallfacer, has users confess their secret plans for humanity's survival. Mostly comedic; occasionally moving.

---

## Round 9 — Days 57-63 (Deepening polarization)

[D] **Day 59**, Political: Brazil withdraws support for the Proposal, citing concerns about global south representation. Lula publishes a long Facebook post explaining.

[D] **Day 61**, Religious: A large schism emerges in US evangelical Christianity. Roughly half the Southern Baptist Convention endorses the Proposal, the other half condemns it as "Babel for the Stars."

[D] **Day 63**, Diplomatic: Russia announces it will build a parallel defense institution if the Wallfacer Project passes without a Russian Wallfacer seat. Widely interpreted as a threat to veto.

[O] **Day 58**, Technology: OpenAI releases a preview of "Wallfacer Advisor" — a tool that lets users argue with an AI that simulates being a Wallfacer. Highly controversial; some users report the tool is "too persuasive."

[A] **Day 62**, Information warfare: Coordinated false leaks about "real" Wallfacer nominee identities flood every major platform. Specific names targeted: a Japanese physicist, an Iranian diplomat, a Kenyan novelist. All three receive death threats.

---

## Round 10 — Days 64-70 (The first nomination rumor)

[D] **Day 65**, Leaked candidate list: A four-name list attributed to the PDC Nominations Subcommittee is leaked by an anonymous source. Names are plausible but unconfirmed. Two accept the premise and issue statements, one declines comment, one denies.

[D] **Day 68**, Activism: Edward Snowden publishes an op-ed titled "I Would Be a Wallbreaker." Argues that true transparency in the Wallfacer era requires a dedicated institutional role for full-spectrum disclosure.

[D] **Day 70**, Regional: Indonesia, the Philippines, Malaysia, and Vietnam issue a joint statement endorsing the Proposal with amendments for Southeast Asian representation. Shifts ASEAN discourse.

[O] **Day 67**, Political: A United States presidential candidate makes "Wallfacer Project Repeal" their central campaign plank. Polling shifts measurable.

---

## Round 11 — Days 71-77 (The backroom deals)

[D] **Day 73**, Diplomatic: Reports surface of backroom negotiations offering permanent Security Council seats to India and Japan in exchange for their endorsement of the Proposal. Both nations publicly deny.

[D] **Day 75**, Official: The PDC Secretariat announces that the proposed Security Council vote will be delayed from October to December 2026 to allow for "additional consultation."

[D] **Day 77**, Religious: The Dalai Lama's succession committee releases a surprise statement that the next Dalai Lama should be raised with knowledge of the Crisis Era as a foundational context. Reshapes conversations in Buddhist-majority regions.

[O] **Day 72**, Activism: A 19-year-old Iranian student becomes the unexpected face of the Global Citizens' Assembly after her Tehran-recorded video goes viral globally. Her arrest by Iranian authorities shifts international sentiment.

---

## Round 12 — Days 78-90 (Convergence toward a provisional verdict)

[D] **Day 80**, Political: France, Germany, and the Netherlands converge on a modified proposal supporting Wallfacers but with a seven-year term rather than life tenure. This "European Compromise" becomes the dominant alternative framing.

[D] **Day 83**, Economic: The Federal Reserve and ECB coordinate on a joint statement that long-horizon defense investments will not be accommodated by monetary policy. Markets rally.

[D] **Day 85**, Activism: The Global Citizens' Assembly announces its virtual referendum results: 41% endorse the Proposal, 38% endorse the European Compromise, 14% endorse the Global Citizens' Alternative, 7% reject any Wallfacer-style framework. Participation: 340 million globally.

[D] **Day 88**, Leaked: A draft UN Security Council resolution circulates. It adopts the European Compromise's seven-year term, adds regional representation quotas, and splits the budget into transparent (60%) and discretionary (40%) tranches.

[D] **Day 90**, Simulation closes on the day the PDC Secretariat announces a formal hearing schedule for late September. The ultimate vote is not held within the simulation window; the outcome prediction is based on momentum, trajectory, and institutional positions.

## Optional Extended Round — Shock Events for Robustness

For robustness runs, seed one of these catastrophic events between rounds 6 and 10:

**Shock A**: A second, distinct Trisolaran signal is detected, contradicting the warning "Do not answer." This second signal has no clear translation.

**Shock B**: A real or fabricated assassination attempt on one of the rumored Wallfacer nominees.

**Shock C**: A sudden public statement from a previously-silent major religious figure (Xi Jinping, the Prophet Muhammad's descendant, a charismatic evangelical) dramatically endorsing or opposing the Proposal.

**Shock D**: A leak revealing that the signal's "pacifist" warning is of deliberately ambiguous authorship — i.e., the signal's message may or may not be honest.

**Shock E**: A major tech company announces the discovery of a practical defense technology that supposedly renders the Wallfacer framework unnecessary.

Each shock should be simulated independently to measure robustness of the 90-day trajectory.
