# The World in April 2026 — Baseline State

This document describes the state of Earth at the moment of the Trisolaran Fleet Confirmation announcement. It is the shared backdrop against which all subsequent events unfold. All actors, institutions, and publics referenced here exist and can speak on social media.

## Geopolitical Landscape

The United States is in the second year of its administration following the 2024 election. Political polarization remains acute; Congress is narrowly divided. China is stable under centralized leadership, pushing aggressive technology sovereignty and Belt & Road digital infrastructure. The European Union has consolidated around a Strategic Autonomy Doctrine following prolonged energy insecurity. Russia continues a frozen conflict in eastern Europe and pivots increasingly toward Asia. India has become the world's most populous democracy and the fastest-growing large economy. Brazil under its current government emphasizes climate leadership.

Tensions flare regularly in the South China Sea, the Taiwan Strait, the Iran-Israel corridor, and between nuclear-armed neighbors India and Pakistan. NATO, SCO, BRICS+, and the G7 remain the dominant bloc structures.

## Technology

Large language models have reached a plateau that skeptics call "AGI deferred" and optimists call "the last mile." OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Tencent, and Alibaba compete openly with models trained on trillion-parameter architectures. Humanoid robotics is mass-producing at scale in Shenzhen and Toyota plants; 200,000 humanoid robots work in warehouses and fast food globally. Brain-computer interfaces from Neuralink and Chinese competitors are implanted in roughly 15,000 patients. Quantum computing has achieved first-useful-application for materials science.

SpaceX operates a fleet of 10,000 Starlink satellites. China's Tiangong station is expanded. A crewed lunar base, jointly US-Japan-ESA, sees continuous occupation since 2025. The James Webb Space Telescope has a decade of data. The Square Kilometre Array is operational in Australia and South Africa.

## Public Mood and Media

Social media is dominated by X, TikTok, Weibo, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, and the fragmented Fediverse. Short-form video is the default medium; algorithmic feeds determine most political opinion formation. Trust in traditional media is at historic lows — around 32 percent globally, lower in Anglophone democracies.

Conspiracy theories about AI, deep state, pandemics, and elite pedophile rings remain mainstream on large platforms. QAnon descendants, anti-vaccine movements, climate denial, and crypto millenarianism all have dedicated audiences in the tens of millions.

Religious observance is rising among Gen Z globally, reversing decades of secularization in some regions. Evangelical Christianity grows in Latin America and sub-Saharan Africa. Orthodoxy revives in Russia and Eastern Europe. Islam remains demographically ascendant. Hindu nationalism is organized at state level in India. New Age, witchcraft, and astrology subcultures dominate young Western women's discourse.

Climate anxiety is pervasive among Western Gen Z, 65 percent of whom report feeling hopeless about the future according to 2025 Pew data.

## Science Context

SETI @ home has been folded into a federated search coordinated by the SETI Institute, Breakthrough Listen (Yuri Milner-funded), and the Chinese FAST radio telescope consortium. No confirmed technosignature has ever been announced before April 2026. The Drake Equation remains a popular lecture topic. The Fermi Paradox is discussed widely in science YouTube and on Lex Fridman's podcast.

Liu Cixin's Three-Body trilogy (三体) is one of the best-selling science fiction works in history. The Netflix adaptation completed three seasons by 2025. Tencent's Chinese adaptation ran for 30 episodes. The phrase "dark forest" has become general-purpose jargon in tech discourse to describe zero-sum competitive environments.

## Key Social Media Platforms as Actors

Each major platform operates its own moderation regime and has an official corporate account that issues statements during crises.

- **X (owned by xAI Corp)**: minimal moderation, Community Notes as primary check
- **Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Threads)**: moderate-to-heavy moderation, Oversight Board
- **TikTok (ByteDance)**: aggressive recommendation, Chinese data firewall
- **Weibo**: Chinese regulator-aligned
- **YouTube (Alphabet)**: brand-safety moderation
- **Reddit**: community-moderated, Content Policy enforcement
- **Telegram**: near-zero moderation
- **Bluesky**: federated, invite-graduated
- **Fediverse (Mastodon instances)**: instance-by-instance governance

## Scientific and Political Bodies

- **United Nations** — especially the Security Council and the new Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA)
- **IAU (International Astronomical Union)** — ultimate naming and verification authority for astronomical discoveries
- **SETI Institute (USA)** — primary non-governmental SETI coordinator
- **Breakthrough Listen** — Yuri Milner-funded, Berkeley-based
- **FAST Consortium (China)** — operates the world's largest filled-aperture radio telescope
- **Square Kilometre Array Observatory (SKAO)** — intergovernmental
- **NASA, ESA, CNSA, Roscosmos, ISRO, JAXA** — national space agencies
- **WHO** — included by precedent as response coordinator for global crises
- **World Economic Forum** — elite convening body
- **Vatican Observatory** — Catholic Church's astronomical voice
- **Al-Azhar (Cairo)** — leading Sunni authority
- **G7, G20, BRICS+, SCO** — political-economic blocs

## The Defining Pre-Crisis Expectation

Before April 2026, no government, religion, or scientific body has a contingency plan for confirmed extraterrestrial intelligence. UNOOSA maintains a post-detection protocol drafted by the IAA (International Academy of Astronautics) but it is advisory only. No constitutional democracy has legislation for the case. No major religion has authoritative doctrine. Fictional literature, cinema, and philosophy are the primary reservoirs of framing.

This is the world into which the Trisolaran signal arrives.
