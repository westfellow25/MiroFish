# The Wallfacer Proposal — Full Text

**Document:** PDC/2026/Draft Resolution 7 — "The Wallfacer Project"
**Released:** July 8, 2026, 14:00 UTC
**Authoring body:** Secretariat of the Planetary Defense Council, with input from the Oxford Future of Humanity Institute, the Santa Fe Institute, the RAND Corporation, the Tsinghua Institute of Strategic Studies, and the French Conseil National du Renseignement.

The following is the text released publicly to all UN member states, with annexes summarized.

---

## Preamble

WHEREAS the confirmation of an approaching Trisolaran fleet constitutes an existential threat to the human species, differing in kind from any prior challenge;

WHEREAS the Trisolaran civilization is presumed to have monitored or will monitor Earth's public communications, deliberations, and scientific disclosures;

WHEREAS conventional defense planning — which relies on transparent doctrine, public debate, and institutional accountability — presumes an adversary who cannot read that same doctrine and those same debates in real time;

WHEREAS open deliberation itself, when conducted on platforms and in forums susceptible to interception, may constitute a strategic handicap of civilization-ending magnitude;

WHEREAS history records no institution capable of maintaining coherent, long-term strategic secrecy under modern democratic conditions across a 400-year time horizon;

WHEREAS the species requires a mechanism of strategic action that simultaneously satisfies four constraints: (1) long-term coherence, (2) adaptive creativity, (3) accountability, and (4) opacity to any external observer;

THE PLANETARY DEFENSE COUNCIL PROPOSES the establishment of the WALLFACER PROJECT as an instrument of strategic response.

---

## Article 1: The Nomination of Wallfacers

1.1 The United Nations Security Council, acting on recommendation from the PDC Secretariat and a panel of scientific advisors, shall nominate four individuals from among Earth's population to serve as WALLFACERS.

1.2 Nominees shall be selected for extraordinary intellectual independence, strategic originality, psychological resilience, and civilizational breadth. No two Wallfacers shall share the same national origin, primary worldview, or institutional affiliation.

1.3 Nominations shall be confirmed by a two-thirds vote of the UN General Assembly, by simple majority of the Security Council, and by the PDC.

## Article 2: Authorities Granted

2.1 Each Wallfacer shall be granted access to any classified or proprietary information of any member state, subject to that state's residual sovereign prerogatives.

2.2 Each Wallfacer shall be granted a personal discretionary budget of no less than 50 billion USD annually, drawn from the Planetary Defense Fund.

2.3 Each Wallfacer shall have the authority to:
   a. commission scientific research from any public or private institution;
   b. request the cooperation of any national military or intelligence service;
   c. instruct the construction of physical infrastructure of up to the equivalent of 200 billion USD per project;
   d. override academic, ethical, and regulatory review processes in matters deemed by the Wallfacer to be mission-critical;
   e. withhold justification for any action from the public, the PDC, national governments, the Security Council, and all other bodies.

2.4 A Wallfacer's actions are subject only to veto by unanimous vote of the PDC in cases of imminent catastrophic risk, subject to further judicial review.

## Article 3: The Principle of Inscrutability

3.1 A Wallfacer shall NOT be required to disclose the purpose, intent, or rationale of any decision.

3.2 A Wallfacer's public statements shall be considered presumptively strategic and may be contradicted by their private actions without constituting perjury, fraud, or breach of public trust.

3.3 All of humanity — including the Wallfacer's family, collaborators, national governments, and the PDC itself — shall proceed under the understanding that any communication from a Wallfacer may be truth, misdirection, or noise.

## Article 4: Wallbreakers

4.1 Upon nomination of each Wallfacer, a designated WALLBREAKER shall be tasked with continuously attempting to deduce the Wallfacer's true strategy.

4.2 Wallbreakers shall have the authority to publish their hypotheses publicly, regardless of impact on the Wallfacer's plans.

4.3 The Wallfacer-Wallbreaker dynamic is understood to be a necessary adversarial process, not a flaw in the system.

## Article 5: Duration and Succession

5.1 A Wallfacer serves for life, or until they voluntarily terminate their mandate.

5.2 Upon the death of a Wallfacer, the Security Council may nominate a successor, but is not required to do so.

5.3 The Project is intended to persist across the 400-year arrival horizon. Inter-generational continuity shall be addressed in Annex IV.

## Article 6: Democratic and Legal Legitimacy

6.1 Recognizing the extraordinary departure this represents from democratic norms, the PDC further proposes:
   a. a universal advisory referendum of UN member state populations on the Wallfacer Project;
   b. ratification by national parliaments or constitutional equivalents;
   c. periodic (every seven years) review by a Wallbreaker-equivalent consultative body.

6.2 Where referendum results conflict with national ratification outcomes, the Security Council is empowered to attempt reconciliation; no binding resolution of such conflicts is provided in this draft.

## Article 7: Transparency Regarding the Framework

7.1 The existence, authorities, and procedural structure of the Wallfacer Project shall be fully public. The opacity concerns plans, not procedure.

7.2 The identities of nominees and Wallfacers shall be public.

7.3 The actions commissioned by Wallfacers — budget allocations, research programs, infrastructure projects — shall be visible in aggregate, though their strategic rationale shall not be.

## Article 8: Additional Considerations

8.1 The PDC Secretariat acknowledges that this proposal represents an unprecedented concentration of discretionary power in four individuals.

8.2 The PDC Secretariat acknowledges that the proposal is susceptible to corruption, capture, and error.

8.3 The PDC Secretariat judges that the alternative — transparent planning against an adversary presumed to observe all planning — is of categorical lesser effectiveness.

8.4 The PDC Secretariat invites public debate on this proposal over a period of no less than 120 days before any Security Council vote.

## Annexes (summarized)

**Annex I — Psychological Assessment Protocols**: methodology for evaluating candidate psychological resilience across a 400-year inter-generational project.

**Annex II — Budget Allocation Framework**: sources, audit mechanisms that do not compromise inscrutability, reserve reconstitution.

**Annex III — Conflict of Interest**: provisions against Wallfacers' personal enrichment; no provisions against Wallfacers' ideological bias.

**Annex IV — Inter-Generational Continuity**: options including serial appointment, inheritance, artificial-intelligence continuity, and trust-based institutional persistence. No option is recommended.

**Annex V — Precedent**: discussion of analogues including WWII Manhattan Project secrecy, Cold War "dead man's switch" doctrines, and the fictional framing in Liu Cixin's "Dark Forest." The Annex acknowledges that the fictional precedent is the primary inspiration and discusses the ethics of constructing an institution from a novel.

**Annex VI — Comparison with Alternatives**: includes transparent international defense programs, accelerated Mars colonization, submission, and do-nothing strategies. Each is assessed on long-term viability.
