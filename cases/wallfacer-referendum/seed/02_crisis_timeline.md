## Week 1 — April 12 to 18, 2026

### April 12 — Reuters (Beijing)

**Chinese FAST Telescope Reports Repeating Signal from Trisolaran Direction**

The National Astronomical Observatories of China announced today that the Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical Telescope (FAST) in Guizhou has detected a repeating narrowband signal originating from the Alpha Centauri system. The signal, first recorded on March 22, has been independently verified by Breakthrough Listen at the Green Bank Telescope. Dr. Li Di, FAST's chief scientist, called the detection "the most important scientific event of the century" but cautioned against premature interpretation.

### April 13 — New York Times

**"It's Really Aliens, This Time" — How Scientists Verified the Trisolaran Signal**

A consortium of astronomers at Caltech, the Max Planck Institute for Radio Astronomy, the SETI Institute, and FAST Consortium jointly published a preprint on arXiv at 03:17 UTC confirming that the signal from the direction of Alpha Centauri cannot be explained by any known natural source. The signal repeats on a precise 1.42 GHz carrier with side-band modulation encoding structured information. Dr. Jill Tarter, emeritus director of the SETI Institute, told the Times: "We knew this day might come. We are completely unprepared for what it means."

### April 14 — Al Jazeera

**Vatican, Al-Azhar, Dalai Lama Call for Calm**

Pope Francis II issued a statement from Castel Gandolfo that "our faith is not diminished by God's larger creation." The Grand Imam of Al-Azhar, Ahmed el-Tayeb, echoed that the Qur'an "does not exclude life on other worlds." The Dalai Lama tweeted: "Compassion extends to all sentient beings, terrestrial or not." Evangelical leaders in the United States were notably divided, with pastor Robert Jeffress suggesting on Fox News that the signal "may be a demonic deception."

### April 16 — CGTN

**President Xi Convenes Emergency Standing Committee; Premier Li to Chair Scientific Response**

In a nationally televised address, President Xi Jinping called the detection "a matter of concern for all humanity" and proposed that "the community of common destiny must rise to this challenge together." China will host an emergency summit in Shanghai on May 5. The European Commission and the United States have already confirmed attendance.

### April 17 — Washington Post

**White House Silent for 72 Hours; Speaker Demands Briefing**

The White House has yet to issue a substantive statement on the Trisolaran signal confirmation. The Speaker of the House demanded an emergency joint session briefing. Sources within the Pentagon confirm that a classified working group was assembled on April 14 under the auspices of the National Security Council.

## Week 2 — April 19 to 25, 2026

### April 20 — BBC

**Signal Decodes to Warning: "Do Not Answer. Do Not Answer. Do Not Answer."**

A multinational team of cryptographers and linguists at Los Alamos, Tsinghua, and the University of Cambridge announced that the modulation of the Trisolaran signal encodes a message in multiple mathematical representations of natural language. The message repeats three times and reads, in plain English translation: "This is a pacifist of your world. Do not answer. Do not answer. Do not answer." The scientific community is split on whether to trust the message's framing.

### April 21 — The Guardian

**Trisolaran Fleet Detected: JWST Infrared Survey Confirms Accelerating Objects**

The James Webb Space Telescope, in an unplanned repointing, has confirmed the presence of approximately one thousand objects in formation emerging from the Alpha Centauri system. The objects exhibit active deceleration signatures inconsistent with any known natural phenomenon. Closing velocity is estimated at 1 percent of the speed of light. Arrival at Earth: approximately 400 years.

### April 22 — Le Monde

**Panic Buying in Seven Capitals; Tokyo Stock Exchange Halts Trading**

Scenes of panic buying were reported in Paris, Tokyo, Mumbai, São Paulo, Berlin, Jakarta, and Mexico City. Gold hit an all-time high of 4,800 USD per ounce. Bitcoin reached 190,000 USD briefly before a sharp reversal. The Tokyo Stock Exchange halted trading for the first time since the 2011 tsunami. The US dollar strengthened against every major currency except the Swiss franc.

### April 23 — Xinhua

**UN General Assembly Emergency Session Opens in New York**

The UN General Assembly convened in emergency session under Article 22 of the Charter. Secretary-General António Guterres addressed the chamber: "Humanity is, for the first time in its history, one species facing one other species. Nothing that separated us yesterday separates us today." Applause lasted four minutes.

### April 24 — The Atlantic (long-form)

**"Dark Forest" Becomes Trending Topic; Liu Cixin in Demand**

Sales of Liu Cixin's Three-Body Problem trilogy have increased 47,000 percent in two weeks. The Netflix adaptation is the number one show in 61 countries. Philosophers, tech founders, and science communicators are debating whether the "dark forest" hypothesis applies to the current situation, given the signal explicitly warned against reply. Liu Cixin, in a rare interview with Xinhua, said: "I wrote fiction. Reality will be different. I hope."

### April 25 — Financial Times

**"Crisis Era" Arrives at Davos; Executives Split on Long-Term Investment**

The World Economic Forum convened an emergency session in Geneva. Leaked executive comments suggest the global economy faces a "400-year problem with no precedent." BlackRock CEO Larry Fink warned against "paralysis by existentialism." Ray Dalio proposed a "Long Horizon Fund" for multi-generational defense projects.
