# Key Organizations — Institutional Actors in the Crisis Era

Organizations with official social media presence and institutional voice. Each is a distinct actor in the simulation.

---

## Intergovernmental Bodies

**United Nations** — primary coordinating authority. Spokesperson accounts include @UN, @antonioguterres, and the newly created @PDC_Official (Planetary Defense Council) and @UNOOSA_Official.

**Planetary Defense Council (PDC)** — newly created on May 5, 2026, under UNSC Resolution 3141. Weighted voting: P5 (US, China, Russia, UK, France) plus rotating permanent seats for India, Brazil, Japan, Germany, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia. Initial 1.2 trillion USD budget. Issues official communiqués every 72 hours during crisis.

**World Health Organization (WHO)** — mental health and pandemic-style response framing. Voice: Tedros.

**International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)** — suddenly relevant as defense technology discussions include fission and fusion options.

**G7, G20, BRICS+, SCO, NATO, AU, ASEAN** — bloc-level communiqués. Each has distinct positioning.

**International Astronomical Union (IAU)** — authoritative scientific voice on signal characteristics and fleet observation.

**UNESCO** — Digital Legacy Project. Cultural heritage preservation.

**Red Cross / Red Crescent** — mental health first aid.

## Social Media Platforms (as Actors)

Each platform issues crisis-response statements, moderation policy updates, and features "verified crisis information" modules.

- **X (xAI Corp)**: Community Notes, minimal moderation, algorithmic amplification.
- **Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, WhatsApp)**: Oversight Board, heavy fact-checking.
- **TikTok (ByteDance)**: Algorithmic recommendation, firewalls across China / rest-of-world.
- **Weibo**: Chinese regulator-aligned.
- **Reddit**: Community moderation, Content Policy.
- **YouTube (Alphabet)**: Brand-safety moderation.
- **Telegram**: Near-zero moderation.
- **Bluesky**: Federated, invite-gated.
- **Mastodon instances**: Federated, per-instance governance.
- **Discord**: Server-by-server.

Platform corporate decisions — whether to label ETO content, whether to algorithmically demote panic, whether to enforce "slow mode" — become simulation-relevant actions.

## The Institutional Landscape in Summary

Over 200 distinct institutional actors will have voices during the simulation. Their behavior is constrained by their mandate, political ideology, funding dependencies, and historical precedent. Individuals inside these institutions may speak in personal capacity (see 03_key_individuals.md). The simulation must capture the interplay between individual voices and institutional voices — how the balance between them shifts as the crisis progresses.
