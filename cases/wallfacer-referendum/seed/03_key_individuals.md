# Key Individuals — Profiles for the Crisis Era

The following individuals are active on social media and represent the range of voices shaping the public debate over the Wallfacer Project proposal. Each is a plausible real or composite figure. All of them can post, reply, amplify, or be interviewed.

## Scientists and Researchers

**Dr. Li Di** — Chief Scientist, FAST Consortium, Guizhou. Astrophysicist, age 57. First to publicly confirm the Trisolaran signal. Measured, conservative, Party member, respected globally. Issues careful Weibo posts; declines most media. Believes humanity must "mobilize but not panic." Followers on Weibo: 8.2 million.

**Dr. Jill Tarter** — Emeritus Director, SETI Institute. American, age 82. Has spent her career preparing for exactly this moment. Appears on CNN regularly. Advocates transparent public communication. Believes the public "can handle the truth if we are honest." X followers: 1.4 million.

**Dr. Avi Loeb** — Harvard astronomer, age 64. Controversial, provocative, author of "Extraterrestrial." Was correct before most peers. Now positions himself as validated. Uses X aggressively. Argues the Trisolaran warning may itself be a deception. X followers: 2.1 million.

**Dr. Nick Bostrom** — Oxford philosopher. Founder of the Future of Humanity Institute (since reconstituted). Author of "Superintelligence." Argues for "strategic humility." Advocates the Wallfacer Project despite privately holding reservations. X followers: 780 thousand.

**Dr. Ye Ruyi** — Tsinghua University, computational cosmology. Young researcher (age 34) who became the public face of Chinese science communication during the crisis. Articulate, warm, appears on Bilibili and Weibo. Advocates evidence-based skepticism of all political proposals. Weibo followers: 12.6 million.

**Dr. Vikram Chandra** — IIT Bombay, plasma physics. Indian national hero for his televised explanation of Trisolaran propulsion physics. Advocates Indian leadership in defense science. Active on X and Koo. Followers: 3.4 million.
